# M4.2 research decision — 2026-10-21

## Decision

**C. NO ADAPTIVE ADVANTAGE DEMONSTRATED.** This is the preregistered rule's result, computed by `src/trusttabx/m4_2_decision.py` and saved in `reports/results/m4_2_research_decision.json`. The default M5 research/engineering strategy is a **source-validation-selected single fixed expert** with its feature preprocessing pipeline and explicit reliability monitoring. The five-expert validation-weighted ensemble is retained as a higher-cost benchmark. Both routers remain research prototypes and must not be the default deployed predictor.

## Evidence used

The previously inspected ACS, hospital, bank and 2025 College final-domain results were treated as exploratory audit evidence. Fresh M4.2 source evaluation re-fit preprocessing and all five experts within each outer source-domain fold and used disjoint inner fit, selection, probability-calibration and threshold-calibration partitions. ACS outer states preserve household disjointness; hospital admission-code folds preserve patient disjointness; bank outer blocks 2 and 3 are forward-only. `docs/M4_2_SOURCE_PROTOCOL.md` and `reports/results/m4_2_nested_*.json` contain index hashes, counts and model costs. Across **13 outer source domains in only three dataset families**, the original metadata router beat the fixed expert in 6 domains. Family-mean Brier (static / metadata router / weighted ensemble): ACS **0.141614 / 0.141437 / 0.141332**; hospital **0.189329 / 0.189170 / 0.188566**; bank **0.056951 / 0.057372 / 0.056665**. The router did not beat both fixed and weighted in every family. The simplified router was also inconsistent. Source confidence thresholds targeting 80% acceptance reached the target in only 9/13 static, 8/13 weighted and 6/13 original-router outer domains. Unlabeled-batch cutoffs achieved fixed 80% acceptance by construction; they are transductive and separate from online fixed thresholds.

On the shopper run, all five expert fits took 3.127 seconds versus 0.393 seconds for the selected random forest alone. Thus the small ensemble Brier gains in this study require about eight times as much measured training time; the five-model inference path also has greater resource demand. These are one-run local CPU timings, not a normalized production cost estimate.

The [locked confirmation](M4_2_CONFIRMATION_PREREGISTRATION.md) was committed in `9d5ad86` before downloading and inspecting UCI shopper labels. Source-selected random forest versus original router Brier was **0.190073 vs 0.196618** on November (2,998 sessions) and **0.103842 vs 0.102092** on December (1,727 sessions). The December router gain over the stronger weighted ensemble was only **0.000442**, below the preregistered 0.002 margin; November lost to both controls. Log loss followed the same broad pattern. Thus the confirmation gate also failed. The related months are one dataset family and cannot serve as two independent family-level replications. Source-only sigmoid calibration improved November Brier to 0.185863 but worsened December to 0.112327; no stable calibration advantage was established.

At source-fixed confidence thresholds, November/December acceptance for fixed RF was **0.7995/0.7817**, and for weighted ensemble **0.8012/0.7759**. The disagreement threshold exceeded 80% on both months but had worse accepted error than fixed confidence on both. No simpler selective policy met the preregistered decision B condition. A batch policy using unlabeled target scores can enforce 80% acceptance, but it is not an online guarantee or equivalent to the source-fixed policy. Full score and risk–coverage metrics are in `reports/results/m4_2_shopper_confirmation.json` and `docs/SHOPPER_MONTH_SHIFT_MANIFEST.md`.

## Statistical and methodological limits

- Three source families and one fresh confirmation family are too few for a credible cross-family confidence interval. Domains within each family and the two shopper months are related; individual rows and repeated model fits are not independent environments. No p-value or broad superiority claim is made.
- The original three-family cross-fit artifacts supplied the two confirmation routers; they contain only source rows. Nested source evaluation re-fit models independently within outer folds, but no hyperparameter search was performed. Ridge alpha and expert settings were locked; this is a finite fixed-configuration comparison, not proof of optimal tuning.
- The shopper dataset has month labels but no exact session timestamps or verifiable user IDs. Page features are retrospective and their pre-checkout timing is not independently certified. The College and bank benchmarks have separate outcome/timing limitations already recorded. These prevent production-use claims, especially in high-stakes settings.
- The fixed expert chosen for a new deployment may differ by dataset. The platform should implement source-selection logic, not hard-code shopper random forest, ACS HistGB or hospital CatBoost globally. Shifted-domain calibration and accepted risk require monitoring and human review; neither conformal coverage nor 80% acceptance is guaranteed under arbitrary shift.

Deployment to real decisions still requires domain-specific data rights, point-in-time feature verification, subgroup review, reliability thresholds and operational validation.
