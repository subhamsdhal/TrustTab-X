# College Scorecard single-cohort institution-type shift

## Provenance and scope

The [TableShift repository](https://github.com/mlfoundations/tableshift) lists `college_scorecard` as a public 124,699-observation benchmark with institution-type shift and official `train`, `validation`, `id_test`, `ood_validation`, and `ood_test` splits. Its [feature code](https://github.com/mlfoundations/tableshift/blob/main/tableshift/datasets/college_scorecard.py) thresholds `C150_4 > 0.5`. The README calls the task “Low Degree Completion Rate,” whereas the feature code's positive class is **high** completion; our target is explicitly `C150_4 > 0.5` and no exact TableShift score is claimed.

We used the [Department of Education's public College Scorecard catalog](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/college-scorecard) distribution `Most-Recent-Cohorts-Institution_05192025.zip`, downloaded 2026-10-21. The catalog metadata states CC BY and describes institution-level completion data; this is the source's licence, not a clearance for every downstream use. URL: `https://ed-public-download.scorecard.network/downloads/Most-Recent-Cohorts-Institution_05192025.zip`. SHA256: `74d464963c2948b7b6e97df6864345055b177466325a2b06c54f0e50e910b8e4`. The 24,026,521-byte ZIP contains a 103,136,944-byte CSV. Raw files stay under ignored `data/raw/college_scorecard/`.

This is a **derived single-cohort benchmark**, not the official TableShift multi-year collection or its official splits. The official package was not installed: we selected a modest public archive so the CPU-only local run was feasible, and locked a new split in `docs/M4_1_PREREGISTRATION.md` before inspecting labels. We retained 2,272 institutions with observed `C150_4`, unique `UNITID`, and `CONTROL` in {1,2,3}. Suppressed and missing target outcomes are excluded, so the evaluated population is selected and may be biased.

## Split and prediction semantics

Public (`CONTROL=1`) and private nonprofit (`2`) institutions form the source; for-profit (`3`) institutions are the single untouched confirmation domain. Stable SHA256 buckets of `UNITID` with seed 264738 split source into fit (1,222), selection (413), probability calibration (222) and threshold calibration (187). Final contains 228 for-profit institutions. Positive rates: fit 0.53519, selection 0.56174, probability calibration 0.54955, threshold calibration 0.58289, final 0.48246. Exact partition institution-ID hashes and CONTROL counts are in `reports/results/college_scorecard_2025_confirmation.json`. No institution appears twice in this single cohort. There are only two source institution-type domains and one final type, so this cannot estimate transfer uncertainty over independent target types.

The 12 preregistered variables are STABBR, HIGHDEG, LOCALE, UGDS, ADM_RATE, SATVRMID, SATMTMID, PCTPELL, TUITIONFEE_IN, AVGFACSAL, PFTFAC, PPTUG_EF. `CONTROL`, identity, and outcome-like fields are excluded. Imputation, encoding and scaling are fit only inside each source-fit expert pipeline. This is retrospective prediction: current institutional descriptors can postdate the entering cohort measured by six-year completion, so prospective decision claims would be unsound. Outcome availability, suppression and missing predictors differ by institution type and may induce selection effects.

## Locked confirmation result

Source-selection Brier chose random forest. On the 228 final institutions, raw fixed RF Brier/log loss were **0.263707/0.724673**. Source-only sigmoid-calibrated RF was **0.266892/0.732861**; uniform ensemble **0.268967/0.738449**; inverse-source-Brier weighted ensemble **0.268696/0.737673**; unchanged cross-family ridge router **0.285757/0.797401**. The router failed the preregistered Brier criterion by 0.022050. It had higher AUROC (0.61294 vs RF 0.56148), illustrating that ranking and proper probability scoring differ. No method is declared better from one small target domain. Five expert fits took 2.865 seconds in this run; peak Windows process working set was 595,964 KiB. Separate router meta-fit time and inference times are in the result JSON; these are one-run measurements, not a hardware-normalized budget.

At the source threshold aiming for 80% decision coverage, raw RF confidence acceptance was only **68.9%** on final institutions with accepted error risk **46.5%**. Disagreement threshold accepted **76.8%**, risk **45.1%**. Router estimated-error threshold accepted **75.4%**, risk **42.4%**; its different coverage prevents a direct superiority claim. The JSON includes target-ranked, matched-coverage risk diagnostics, risk–coverage points and coverage at 10%/20% risk limits. Those target-ranked values are retrospective diagnostics and cannot be used to choose deployment thresholds. No arbitrary-shift coverage guarantee applies.

Reproduce locally with `python -c "from trusttabx.college_scorecard import run_college_confirmation,save_college_result; save_college_result(run_college_confirmation(),'reports/results/college_scorecard_2025_confirmation.json')"` after downloading the pinned archive into its ignored path and installing `.[dev,benchmarks]` plus psutil. The result file records the archive hash, split hashes, five expert costs, policy metrics and selective diagnostics.
