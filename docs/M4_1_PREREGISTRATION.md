## Independent confirmation family

Use the public Department of Education 2025 most-recent institution-level cohort archive `Most-Recent-Cohorts-Institution_05192025.zip`. This is a **single-cohort, TableShift-inspired derivative**, not the official multi-year TableShift task or its exact official splits. One row is one institution. Target: `C150_4 > 0.5`, matching TableShift's low/completion threshold direction only after checking its code (`>0.5` is the positive class). Retain rows with numeric, observed `C150_4` and `CONTROL` in {1,2,3}. Public and private nonprofit institutions (`CONTROL` 1,2) are source; for-profit (`CONTROL` 3) is one untouched confirmation domain. Drop duplicate `UNITID` and rows without it before partitioning; never let one institution cross partitions. If one class is absent or a partition has fewer than 100 observations, report infeasible without changing these rules.

Use a SHA256-based deterministic `UNITID` hash with seed 264738 to split source institutions: 60% fit, 20% selection, 20% calibration. Neither target labels nor target features enter fit, selection, calibration or adaptation. Five existing fixed experts and preprocessing are fitted only on source fit. Selection Brier chooses one fixed expert and inverse-Brier ensemble weights. Calibration is reserved for source-only sigmoid probability calibration and 80%-coverage uncertainty thresholds. Final target is evaluated once. Fixed raw expert, calibrated fixed expert, uniform and weighted probability ensembles, and the **unchanged** ridge router trained on ACS/hospital/bank source cross-fit rows are the predeclared prediction policies. For each policy assess confidence and ensemble-disagreement rejection; router's estimated squared error is a third rejection score for router. Source-calibration quantiles set cutoffs, with realized target coverage always reported. At equal *realized* target coverage, ranking comparisons are descriptive diagnostics only, since using target scores to match counts is transductive and cannot set deployed thresholds.

Exclude `CONTROL`, institution IDs, all completion/retention/repayment/earnings fields, and any directly derived outcome. Numeric missing values are imputed inside source-fit pipelines. This remains a *retrospective association* task: contemporaneous institutional covariates may be measured after the entrant cohort's start. No prospective claim follows.

Primary objective is mean Brier, secondary log loss and AUROC/AUPRC. A method succeeds only if its Brier is lower than source-selected fixed raw expert on the new target, with no worse log loss by more than 0.01; report absolute differences and source/final domain rates. Separate selective objective is error among accepted predictions at source-calibrated nominal 80% decision coverage; report actual target coverage and risk. Compare risk at matched target coverage only as a ranking diagnostic. No single domain result establishes generality; no row-level significance test. Record fit/inference time and peak process working set. Do not retune after seeing target results.

## Source-only diagnosis and validation

The existing three family source-crossfit artifacts may be used to diagnose errors, prediction diversity, correlations and proxy oracle routing headroom. Leave one **entire family** out when fitting the unchanged ridge router on the other two families' out-of-domain source predictions. Evaluate on the held-out family's source cross-fit predictions, grouped by domain. This is exploratory meta-transfer validation, not strict fresh confirmation: base learners for individual held-out domains may have used other domains, and the three final outcomes were already viewed. No held-out family labels enter router training or global fixed-expert selection. A family-level interval with three families is too unstable for a confirmatory inference.

No target-label shift correction or unsupervised adaptation will be performed at this checkpoint. Prevalence differences alone do not prove prior shift. The College Scorecard final target is reserved for this single locked method comparison.
