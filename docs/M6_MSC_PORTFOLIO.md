# MSc Data Science portfolio summary and research statement

## Project summary

**TrustTab-X — trustworthy tabular machine learning under distribution shift.** Built a reproducible research framework and a secured local ACSIncome decision-support demonstration. The work joins experimental design, leakage-safe split roles, calibration and selective prediction diagnostics, natural-domain evaluation, negative-result reporting and practical API reliability. In bounded ACS, hospital and bank studies, the recorded fixed expert degraded under geographic, admission-domain and temporal changes. A nested source-domain router and a preregistered new shopper family did not establish a robust adaptive advantage; the fixed expert remained the selected policy. M5.1 is a local demo only. [Manuscript](../reports/paper/TrustTab-X-manuscript.md), [claim trace](M6_CLAIM_TRACEABILITY.md), [reproducibility](M6_REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

## Research statement

I am interested in reliable machine learning when the training population differs from the decision population. This project began with synthetic and OpenML baselines, then moved to natural geographic, hospital-domain and forward temporal splits. I designed independent fit, selection, calibration and test roles and audited household/patient leakage and bank feature timing. I evaluated probability quality with Brier score, calibration diagnostics, and selective error under source-fixed acceptance thresholds. The adaptive-routing hypothesis was compelling but did not survive the locked research gate: weighted ensembles were competitive or better, source-fixed coverage transported inconsistently, and the new shopper family failed the preregistered gain margin. I retained the negative result and implemented a narrower fixed-expert local inference artifact with authentication, schema checks and drift monitoring. This reinforced my interest in statistical evaluation under shift, honest uncertainty, reproducible science and the translation of a model into a governed system.

## Methods and learning evidence

- Research: preregistration, domain-held-out/nested evaluation, source-only selection, calibration, source-fixed selective thresholds, negative-result interpretation.
- Engineering: Python package, tracked aggregate evidence, versioned model contract, FastAPI local service, authentication, dashboard, audit metadata, smoke and unit testing.
- Limits: only a few independent natural families; bounded modified ACS/hospital protocols; bank customer recurrence and event-time uncertainty; no target-independent adaptive gain; Docker and internal governance gate open.
