# Exploratory M4 reliability router: locked protocol

Recorded 2026-10-20 after source-only cross-fitting and before any router final-domain evaluation. The three families are 2018 ACS geography, UCI hospital admission source and UCI bank chronology. Source-only cross-fitting produced 7 held-out states, 4 patient-disjoint admission-source-code groups and 3 forward temporal blocks. This suffices for an exploratory **within-family unseen-domain** router evaluation, not a claim of generalisation to a new dataset family. The final New England, emergency-admission and late-bank test labels are excluded from fitting and policy decisions.

Five fixed experts are re-fit on each family's source fit partition. The router receives only five probabilities, five binary entropies, mean probability and probability standard deviation (12 common deployment-available metadata values). It never receives raw ACS, hospital or bank predictors, target labels, target test metrics or future batch labels. No target-domain shift statistic is used in this first prototype; that is an explicit ablation against the planned shift-aware method.

Fit a `StandardScaler` and five `Ridge(alpha=100)` conditional squared-error models on source-domain-held-out predictions, one per expert. Each of the three dataset families receives equal total weight, and each source environment within a family receives equal weight. At inference choose the expert with minimum predicted squared error, clipping estimated error to [0,1]. The abstention score is that predicted error. There is no hyperparameter search or target-label adaptation.

For each family, source selection rows select the best fixed expert by Brier and determine inverse-Brier weights for the five-expert validation-weighted ensemble. Further controls are the uniform mean and a simple expert selector taking the highest single-expert confidence. All five experts are available to the router and ensemble controls at equal upstream fitting cost; the static single-expert deployment cost is reported separately. Source calibration rows (disjoint from cross-fit and selection) set each strategy's split-conformal threshold at alpha 0.1 and a risk/uncertainty cutoff aiming for 80% source calibration decision coverage. Final-domain risk is evaluated at the resulting actual coverage; no target threshold adjustment is permitted. Report Brier, log loss, AUROC, AUPRC, 10-bin ECE, empirical conformal coverage, and ranked risk–coverage/AURC. Differences across three families are descriptive; no row-level p-value or universal coverage claim.

This initial protocol does **not** satisfy the full shift-aware contribution: it has no explicit feature-distribution shift descriptor and only three families. If it fails, preserve the negative result. A later shift-aware router needs further pre-registered environments and nested family/domain-held-out selection; do not tune this prototype after viewing final results.
