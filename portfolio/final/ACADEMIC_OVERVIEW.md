# TrustTab-X | One-page research overview

**Project:** Trustworthy tabular machine learning under distribution shift. **Status:** completed research portfolio and secured local demonstration; no peer-reviewed publication or production deployment. Developed for a Growtern Academy portfolio upgrade, as reported by the project author; no employment, supervision or endorsement is inferred.

**Research question.** When a binary tabular predictor is evaluated on a different geographic population, hospital admission group or future period, how do probability quality and selective acceptance change? Can a source-trained expert router beat a source-selected fixed expert and a weighted ensemble without viewing final target labels?

**Design.** Five classical candidates (logistic regression, histogram gradient boosting, random forest, XGBoost and CatBoost) were evaluated with separated source fit, selection, calibration and threshold-calibration roles. ACS partitions were household-disjoint; hospital partitions were patient-disjoint; bank evaluation was forward-only. Nested outer-source-domain validation refit candidates and router while excluding the outer domain. A new shopper family was preregistered before final labels were viewed. Brier score was the primary probability metric; calibration and source-fixed selective acceptance were secondary. ACS and hospital studies are bounded modified protocols, not official TableShift scores.

**Evidence.** Selected expert Brier was **0.131644/0.145517** on ACS ID/OOD, **0.201603/0.250504** on hospital ID/OOD and **0.063753/0.337574** on bank near/later future, where prevalence also changed sharply. The router beat static in **6/13 correlated source domains**, while weighted ensemble family means were lower. On the fresh shopper family, the December router gain over weighted was **0.000442**, below the preregistered **0.002** margin, and November was worse. Source-fixed 80% acceptance frequently failed to transport. **M4.2 decision C: no adaptive advantage demonstrated.** Only a few independent families exist, so no family-level confidence interval or broad superiority claim is made.

**Engineering.** The supported local ACS pipeline is a source-selected fixed histogram gradient boosting expert. Its manifest pins feature schema, data/split hashes and pipeline SHA-256; a separately approved manifest digest gates local loading. Authenticated FastAPI endpoints validate records, score fixed predictions, report feature drift, and calculate admin-only delayed-label metrics. The dashboard and privacy-conscious audit metadata were checked locally. **M5.1 release B: local demo only.** Docker execution, TLS, managed identity, central audit governance and data-rights/security approval remain open.

**Data Science contribution.** The project demonstrates proper scoring-rule evaluation, calibration under shift, nested and domain-aware validation, careful leakage assumptions, probabilistic monitoring, compute trade-offs and transparent negative-result interpretation. It does not assert novelty, admission eligibility or operational safety.
