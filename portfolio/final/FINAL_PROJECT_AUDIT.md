# Final TrustTab-X project audit — M7

**Date:** 21 October 2026. **Branch:** `codex/trusttabx-autopilot`. **Portfolio scope:** academic/placement review of a completed local research demonstration. **Research decision:** M4.2 **C — no adaptive advantage demonstrated**. **Engineering release:** M5.1 **B — local demo only**.

## What was built

The repository contains a Python benchmarking package, synthetic and OpenML pilots, bounded natural geographic/hospital/temporal studies, source-domain cross-fitting, a nested router comparison, a preregistered additional shopper family, reliability and selective-risk diagnostics, and an ACS fixed-expert artifact and FastAPI demonstration. The service validates a separately approved manifest digest, schema, artifact hash and version; supports operator/admin roles, drift and delayed-label endpoints, in-memory telemetry, a local dashboard and privacy-conscious HTTP audit metadata. The research and engineering history remains intact.

M7 produced a six-page A4 [PDF manuscript](TrustTab-X-manuscript.pdf) from the [editable Markdown source](TrustTab-X-manuscript.md) using a Python/ReportLab builder. All six pages were rendered to PNG and visually inspected for missing figures, clipped equations, broken tables and unreadable text. The full [review package](README.md) includes the manuscript, six scientific figures, experimental summary, one-page academic overview, reproducibility/traceability, case study and architecture diagram, Growtern context, resume bullets, technical Q&A and local demo walkthrough. It excludes raw data, model binary, credentials and local audit logs.

## Scientific conclusion and critical review

The ACS bounded geographic fixed-expert ID/OOD Brier was 0.131644/0.145517; hospital modified patient-disjoint ID/OOD was 0.201603/0.250504; bank near/later future was 0.063753/0.337574 with a large prevalence change. These are distinct study designs and are not pooled or official TableShift scores. Nested source evaluation found the router better than static in 6/13 correlated source domains; weighted ensemble family means were lower. In the preregistered shopper family, November was worse and December's router gain over weighted (0.000442) missed the locked 0.002 margin. Source-fixed acceptance often missed 80% on target domains. The appropriate result is a negative router gate and a fixed-expert engineering baseline.

The manuscript's equations were checked against `src/trusttabx/reliability.py`: the router predicts per-expert squared error with fixed-alpha ridge regressors and selects one expert per row; it does not learn mixture weights. The weighted control uses inverse source-selection Brier weights. The static policy is raw source-selected output; calibrated static is a separately reported control. A citation title was corrected to the official NeurIPS TableShift record. Existing algorithms, Brier score, calibration and benchmark methods are cited as prior work; no new estimator, statistical significance, peer review or publication is claimed.

ACS household and hospital patient disjointness reduce entity leakage. Bank customer recurrence and exact feature times cannot be independently verified. The 13 outer source domains represent only three original families, and November/December are one correlated shopper family; there is no credible family-level confidence interval. Aggregate ECE and drift alerts do not prove subgroup reliability or individual error. Retrospective target-ranked curves cannot set deployed acceptance thresholds. These limits are stated in the paper and portfolio.
