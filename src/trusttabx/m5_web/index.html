02 / Interpretation

Why the router stayed a research prototype

13Nested source domains across three families

The source-selected fixed expert remains the default for the local ACSIncome service. A small difference in one dataset or month is not a consistent advantage across independent settings.

Leakage-aware protocol

Disjoint fit, selection, probability calibration and final evaluation partitions. Temporal and group boundaries are respected where the data support them.

Interpret scores carefully

ID/OOD, future windows and single held-out cohorts answer different questions. Calibration and selective coverage under arbitrary shift are empirical, not guarantees.

Scope & reproducibility

Raw restricted data and model weights are not shipped with this explorer. The completed manuscript records methods and limitations.

Research finding & limitations

The M4.2 study found no consistent adaptive-routing advantage over the source-selected fixed expert or simple ensembles. This is an offline decision-support demo, not an approved system for employment, credit, medical or other high-stakes decisions. Data and features are specific to ACSIncome 2018; incompatible datasets require their own model and schema.
